We are pleased to announce that Verkada will be hosting the 2027 Fremont, Peninsula and San Mateo County Chapter Competitions at their headquarters in downtown San Mateo:

Verkada HQ  
406 E 3rd Ave, San Mateo, CA 94401

- **San Mateo County:** Saturday, February 27, 2027
- **Fremont and Peninsula:** Sunday, February 28, 2027

We are grateful to Verkada for their continued support of MATHCOUNTS.

If your chapter's date does not work for your school, please [let us know](@/_index.md) as soon as possible so that we can look into alternative testing arrangements.

We will post the schedule and other logistics on the logistics pages once they are set.

If your school hasn't registered for MATHCOUNTS yet, please register at [mathcounts.org/register](https://www.mathcounts.org/register) by December 15.

Please let us know if you have any questions in the meantime!
