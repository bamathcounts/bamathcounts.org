Register through the [MATHCOUNTS Foundation](https://www.mathcounts.org/register). Its registration page requires an account. Students must register in advance; there is no walk-in participation.

## Schools

A school can register one team of up to four students and up to ten additional individual competitors, for a maximum of 14. Confirm your school’s chapter with the coordinator before registering.

- **Regular school deadline:** December 15, 2026, at 11:59 p.m. Pacific.
- **School fee:** $48 per competitor, or $53 after the regular deadline.
- **Non-school competitor fee:** $75, or $85 after the regular deadline.

Eligible Title I schools may qualify for a 50% discount. See the Foundation’s [registration information](https://www.mathcounts.org/register) for the current terms.

## Students and families

Ask your math teacher or school administration about participating. If your school will not register or support participation, a parent or guardian may register an eligible **non-school competitor (NSC)** after confirming this with school officials.

Read the [official rules and procedures](https://www.mathcounts.org/programs/official-rules-procedures) for eligibility, rounds and calculator rules.

## Practice

The Foundation’s [Past Competitions](https://www.mathcounts.org/resources/past-competitions) provide free problems and answers. The [Problem of the Week](https://www.mathcounts.org/resources/problem-week-archive) is another practice resource.
