2026 Peninsula Chapter logistics

Historical reference · Saturday, February 7, 2026

This event has ended. These are last season’s arrangements, based on the logistics published February 2, 2026. Dates, venue, access rules, lunch and advancement may differ in 2027. Current-season information.

Location

The 2026 competition was held at Verkada, 406 East 3rd Avenue, San Mateo, CA 94401.

The published schedule was subject to change. Check-in and entry began at 9:00 a.m.; participants were asked not to arrive earlier.

The official competition rounds were before lunch. Afternoon activities were optional, and students with scheduling conflicts could leave after the Team Round.

Venue registration, arrival and parking

Students and volunteers were required to complete venue visitor registration. Registration for this past event is closed.

  • Student drop-off and pickup were at the Third Avenue entrance.
  • Parents who were not coaches or volunteers could not remain in the venue or use the garage.
  • Visitors checked in at the front desk, collected a name tag and were escorted to the first-floor event space. Students could not wait in the lobby after collecting their tag.
  • Coaches and volunteers could park in the garage next to the main entrance. It opened at 8:00 a.m.; security provided elevator access.
  • Participants had access to first-floor bathrooms, water fountains, drinks and snacks.

Lunch

Students were offered two slices of pizza and a bottle of water, or could bring their own lunch. An optional $5 lunch donation was requested.

Volunteering

The 2026 organizers invited coaches and other volunteers to proctor, especially the Target Round. Anyone affiliated with a competitor could proctor but could not grade exams. For new opportunities, see current volunteer information.

2026 competition and advancement

Countdown was unofficial and did not affect scores or state advancement. The top two teams and the top three individuals not on those advancing teams qualified for the State Competition from the Peninsula Chapter.

These were the published 2026 policies, not a confirmation of 2027 advancement quotas.
