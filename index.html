Announcements

Registration

Register through the MATHCOUNTS Foundation. This opens a sign-in or create-account page. Students must be registered in advance; there is no walk-in participation.

Students and parents

Ask your math teacher or school administration about participating. Schools may register one team of up to four students and up to ten additional individual competitors, for a maximum of 14.

If your school will not register or support participation through the school, a parent or guardian may register an eligible non-school competitor (NSC). Confirm this with school officials first. NSCs compete individually, not in the Team Round. Home-school and virtual-school students should follow the official eligibility rules.

Coaches and new schools

  1. Get school approval and choose a representative: a teacher, administrator or approved parent volunteer.
  2. Confirm your chapter with its coordinator, then create a Foundation account and register the school.
  3. Select up to four team members and up to ten individuals.
  4. Submit names and complete roster changes before your chapter’s roster lockout. Confirm that deadline with the coordinator.

The national competition format takes approximately three hours; this is not a confirmed local start-to-finish schedule. Any afternoon activities, Countdown Round and awards times will be listed in the local schedule.

What to bring

  • Pencils, pens and erasers are allowed. Organizers provide pencils and paper. Do not bring your own scratch or graph paper.
  • Bring a permitted calculator for Target, Team and eligible tiebreakers. Calculators cannot have a QWERTY keypad, Wi-Fi or a wireless adapter. Phones, tablets and other smart devices cannot be used as calculators.
  • No calculators in Sprint or Countdown. Notes and reference materials are not permitted.

All competitors take Sprint and Target. Only school team members take Team. A local Countdown Round is optional and has not been confirmed.

The winning chapter team and at least the two highest-ranked competitors outside that team advance to state. Additional qualifiers follow the state coordinator’s policy.
