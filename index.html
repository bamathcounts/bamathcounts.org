More from MATHCOUNTS ↗ (opens in a new tab)
What is MATHCOUNTS?+

MATHCOUNTS is a national middle school math program. Its Competition Series gives students in grades 6–8 the chance to build problem-solving skills and compete alongside their peers, progressing through school, chapter, state, and national levels.

When and where are the 2027 competitions?+

Dates and venues for our 2027 chapter competitions have not been confirmed. We’ll update this website with the details and competition-day schedules once they’re ready.

How do I register?+

Registration is through the MATHCOUNTS Foundation, not this website. Ask your school’s coach about participating. If your school does not participate, check the Foundation’s non-school competitor eligibility and registration information.

Visit MATHCOUNTS registration ↗ (opens in a new tab)
What does the competition look like?+

The written competition includes the individual Sprint and Target Rounds and a collaborative Team Round. The Sprint Round has 30 problems in 40 minutes without calculators; Target has four pairs of problems with six minutes per pair; Team has 10 problems in 20 minutes. Calculators are permitted in Target and Team. The fast-paced Countdown Round is optional at the chapter level; our local format will be announced with the event details.

Do I need to be a math expert to get started?+

You can start practicing wherever you are. Try a few handbook problems, work with a friend, and ask your school’s coach about the program. For competition eligibility and participation requirements, refer to the official MATHCOUNTS rules.
